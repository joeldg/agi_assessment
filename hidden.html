How today's figure is derived

Re-derived under definitions v2.0, the Index stays at 2.5% (its parts sum to about 2.3): A about 0.55 (lab term 1.4% × kept-quiet factor 0.6 + state term 0.1%, times 0.57), hidden self-improvement outside A about 1.7 (B is unchanged, but less of it overlaps the smaller A) and C or D outside A about 0.05, where the 0.57 factor is our chance that a system meeting the v1.0 parts also meets the parts v2.0 adds, held down most by generalization to new tasks today and by learning on the job later.

The four hidden scenarios, and one open one

Definitions v2.0: "AGI" means AGI as defined on Is AGI here? The Index counts A to D only; D-open is open by definition.

  • A · Hidden AGI: 0.5% today · 19% by end-2030 · 31% by end-2035

    A system meets our definition of AGI (definitions v2.0), and whoever built it, a company or a government program, has kept that level of capability from the public for at least 30 days. Admitting that an unreleased model exists doesn't count as disclosure.

  • B · Hidden self-improvement: 2% today · 22% by end-2030 · 33% by end-2035

    An AI system does most of the work of building a more capable successor, with at least a 3x speed-up over humans alone, and whoever runs it, a company or a government program, has kept this from the public for at least 30 days. Partial AI-driven acceleration is already public, so it doesn't count. B does not need AGI.

  • C · Covert AGI actor: 0.3% today · 7.5% by end-2030 · 13% by end-2035

    An AGI-level system takes sustained actions online or in the economy, and the public hasn't known for at least 30 days, on either of two paths: sanctioned but undisclosed (its developer or another operator runs it on purpose and doesn't say so), or rogue or stolen (it acts outside its developer's control). Agent incidents below AGI level are warning signs, not proof.

  • D · Covert government influence: 0.1% today · 3% by end-2030 · 7% by end-2035

    Output from an AGI-level system materially shapes a major government decision without the public knowing. Heavy government use of tools below AGI level doesn't count.

  • D-open · Open government influence: 0.2% today · 23% by end-2030 · 45% by end-2035

    The same through acknowledged government use, even of a system whose AGI-level capability hasn't been disclosed. It is outside the Index, which counts only hidden scenarios.
