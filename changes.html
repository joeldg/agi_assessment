Method

We change the version for any change to a definition, a gauge, the Index formula or a threshold. How we work: Method.

  • v2.0 · Oct 6, 2026 · Definitions v2.0 replace the v1.0 strict AGI bar wherever we use it: the 'AGI anywhere' forecast, hypotheses A, C, D and D-open, and alarm condition Y1. Our working definition: AGI is a system with the full scope of human cognitive autonomy: it can learn, reason, plan and generalize knowledge across any intellectual domain, without task-specific retraining. Physical and embodied work is out of scope. The v1.0 remote-work test (reliably, 80% or better, doing at least 80% of economically valuable remote professional tasks at the level of a median skilled professional, including multi-week projects, with no task-specific human scaffolding) stays as one of its tests, and we track the definition in eight parts with published tests on agi.html: breadth, quality, reliability, reasoning, planning and long projects, autonomy, learning on the job and generalization. v2.0 keeps every v1.0 requirement and adds learning on the job, generalization to new kinds of problem and open-ended reasoning, so we re-derived every number that needs an AGI-level system as its v1.0 value times our probability that a system meeting the remote-work test by that date also meets the rest of the definition: about 0.57 today, 0.77 by end-2030 and 0.89 by end-2035; of the parts v2.0 adds, generalization to new tasks holds the factor down most today, and learning on the job later. Today / by end-2030 / by end-2035, AGI anywhere (public or hidden) goes from 1.5% / 45% / 65% to 0.9% / 35% / 58%; A from 1% / 25% / 35% to 0.5% / 19% / 31%; C from 0.5% / 10% / 15% to 0.3% / 7.5% / 13%; D from 0.25% / 4% / 8% to 0.1% / 3% / 7%; and D-open from 0.3% / 30% / 50% to 0.2% / 23% / 45%. B doesn't depend on the AGI bar and stays at 2% / 22% / 33%, and the Hidden AGI Index stays at 2.5% (about 2.3 before rounding, down from about 2.5), most of it B. The other inputs are unchanged: the chance that an unreleased lab system meets the remote-work test, the chance it has been kept quiet for 30 days, the state-program term, and the shares of C, D and D-open. These moves come from the change of definition, not from news. Readings and forecasts published under v1.0 keep their meaning and resolve under v1.0. Y1 is restated on the new bar in its own alarm-criteria changelog entry (Y2, which uses Y1's bar, follows), and no other threshold changes. Why: Readers couldn't see how our numbers identify AGI, and the v1.0 bar named only a remote-work test. v2.0 says what we mean by AGI, adds the abilities most outside definitions treat as central (learning on the job and handling new kinds of problem), and splits the definition into parts we can measure and show.
    Definitions v2.0: the definition and the conversion factors

    AGI: a system with the full scope of human cognitive autonomy: it can learn, reason, plan and generalize knowledge across any intellectual domain, without task-specific retraining.

    Conversion factors (the chance that a system meeting the v1.0 test also meets the rest of the definition): 0.57 today · 0.77 by end-2030 · 0.89 by end-2035.

    How the next reading converts our numbers: At the next reading, every number that needs an AGI-level system is re-derived through its published derivation with the factor applied to the chance of meeting the rest of the definition: AGI anywhere and A from their unrounded v1.0 inputs (today: lab term 1.4 and state term 0.1 times k; A also times the unchanged 0.6 kept-quiet factor); by end-2030 and end-2035 each v1.0 value times k; C, D and D-open keep their published shares of AGI; B is unchanged; the Index is re-derived from its parts; everything is rounded once to our grid (half up). Evidence moves that day are reported separately.

  • v1.1 · Sep 30, 2026 · Level 3 (Alarm) needs convergent proof, not official confirmation: at least three independent lines of evidence from different modalities that share no original source, every fact authenticated, no single innocent explanation that covers all the lines, and a unanimous adversarial tribunal. The format of a Level-3 alert and its evidence page is published in advance. Why: Being wrong at Alarm would cost the site its credibility, but waiting for a developer to confirm would make the alarm pointless, because by then the secret is out.
  • v1.1 · Sep 30, 2026 · Fire-alarm criteria v1.1: a symmetric exit rule (a level drops after its rule has gone unsatisfied for 30 consecutive days, to the level the rules then give); W1 counts only a measured or credibly reported capability gap; W5 is a rate (a rise of 10 points or more within 6 months in a lab's published AI-led share of its AI R&D); borderline values don't count; W6 counts only days of internal use, so a safety pause suspends it, while W2 keeps running and one model can't count twice; X2 uses residuals (3 residual SDs above the trend fit) on an instrument that can measure them; X3 counts published estimates; X4 is measured against Epoch AI's trend; Y1 names both halves of the strict bar; every trigger says when it clears. Every level change gets a breaking alert that a person approves before it is sent, and the daily and weekly issues are never held. The level stays Watch, on W3, W4 and W5. Why: Under v1.0 one rising trigger could hold Watch for good, W1 rested on our own estimate at exactly its threshold, X2 couldn't be observed and X4 could fire on normal build-to-release lags. Fixed while no level change is being considered; the details are in the alarm changelog.

  • v1.2 · Oct 6, 2026 · Y1 restated on definitions v2.0: an undisclosed system must meet AGI as defined on agi.html, on evidence equivalent to passing all eight parts' tests at once, including both halves of the earlier remote-work test; physical and embodied work is out of scope. Y2 refers to Y1's bar and follows it. No other condition, threshold, level or rule changes. Display only, not criteria: each condition gains a short label. Re-evaluated the same day: the level stays Level 1 · Watch on W3, W4 and W5; no Y condition is met and no case is open. Why: Definitions v2.0 (method v2.0, same date) replace the strict bar everywhere, so the alarm's top level and our numbers use one definition of AGI. Made while no level change is being considered, as our rules require.
  • v1.1 · Sep 30, 2026 · Exit rule made symmetric: a level drops after its rule has gone unsatisfied for 30 consecutive days, to the level the rules then give. W1 counts only a measured or credibly reported gap, not our estimate. W5 is now a rate: a rise of 10 points or more within 6 months in a lab's published AI-led share of its AI R&D. Borderline values don't count. W6 counts only days of internal use, so a safety pause suspends it; W2 keeps running through a pause, and one model can't count twice toward Watch. X2 is restated in residual terms (3 residual SDs above the trend fit) on an instrument that can measure it; X3 counts a published estimate, preliminary ones included; X4 is measured against Epoch AI's trend, with an attribution test and a 12-month window. Y1 names both halves of our strict bar. Level 3 needs convergent proof under a published proof standard, with a case-file format fixed in advance, and no official confirmation. Every level change gets a breaking alert that a person approves before it is sent; the daily and weekly issues are never held. Every trigger now says when it clears. Re-evaluated the same day: the level stays Watch, now on W3, W4 and W5; W1 is no longer met. Why: Under v1.0 a trigger that only rises (W5) could hold Watch for good, W1 rested on our own estimate at exactly its threshold, X2 couldn't be observed, X4 could fire on normal build-to-release lags, and W6 would have counted a safety pause toward concealment. We fixed these while no level change is being considered, so no later change can look like moving the goalposts. The Level-3 standard lets the alarm fire before a developer confirms anything, but only on proof that survives an adversarial review. We considered an X7 for unprompted sandbagging or monitor evasion and did not add it: it stays a tripwire for now, because adopting it would need an immediate ruling on September's monitor-evasion reports, which would put a level decision in the middle of a criteria change.
