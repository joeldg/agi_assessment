Step 4: Reasoning discipline

First, a lab's own measure of its own automation is a self-report: the 11.9% figure and the no-acceleration finding come from the same document, so we use the definitions in it rather than the headline number. Second, each quote from the Oct 13 Commons session rests on one outlet (only OpenAI's withholding of GPT-6.1 Astra is reported by two outlets, in different words) until the committee publishes a transcript, so none moves a number on its own. Third, Google's safety audits can only count what its monitors flagged or the model verbalized, so zero findings are a floor, not proof of absence (our inference on each).
