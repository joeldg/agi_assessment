For hidden capability or hidden self-improvement

  • PSI says its research system, using instances of a non-public Anthropic model, had a complete proof of a conjecture open since 2008 during the third day of its run, improved it twice, and that Lean accepted the final version (PSI; verified fact), which Stanford specialists call 'a huge result' (the Stanford researchers, via PSI; expert opinion).

Against

  • Claude Mythos 5.1, the same model as the generally available Fable 5.1 with different safeguards, is available only through Anthropic's trusted access programs (Anthropic; verified fact), so 'non-public' need not mean hidden capability (our inference).
