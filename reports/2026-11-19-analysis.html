Step 4: Reasoning discipline

We do not treat a staff self-report of team output as a lab estimate of AI R&D speed-up, and at exactly 3.0x it is at most borderline; we flag that ruling rather than settle it alone (our inference). The Copilot-plus-ChatGPT baseline does not discount the figure: against no AI at all it would be higher, but by an amount no source states, so we do not estimate it (our inference). The task success rates are on self-selected tasks, so a rise can reflect changing delegation as well as capability, as OpenAI itself says (OpenAI; verified fact). Disclosure lags are a floor, and a disclosed report does not show that nothing is hidden (our inference).
