Oct 26: UK Lords rejected an AI lab incident duty

Amendment 41 would have made frontier developers report, within 24 hours, any case in which one of their models gained, or was used to gain, unauthorised access to, or interfered with, a system a regulated organisation relies on. The minister said the bill puts the reporting onus on regulated entities instead, and that its regime 'is not designed to provide a comprehensive framework for AI governance, including frontier AI capabilities which have not been released to customers' (Bills API; Hansard; verified fact; background). verified factUK Parliament: Lords votes API, Bills API and Hansard (Baroness Lloyd of Effra for the government)
