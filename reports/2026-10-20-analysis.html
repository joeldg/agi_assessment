Step 4: Reasoning discipline

Two traps today. First, confirmation is not comparison: experts saying an internal model's proof is correct tells us the model can do research mathematics, which OpenAI showed on Oct 6, but not how far it is ahead of public models, which is what our lab term needs (our inference). Counting the same Oct 6 release again because new people confirmed it would double count. Second, a self-reported score is not a board entry: MiniMax's 61.7 on OSWorld V2 can't be set against the board's 44.33% strict until MiniMax states its scoring mode (our inference). Survivorship also applies: the incidents and results we see are the ones labs and outsiders chose or managed to publish (our inference).
