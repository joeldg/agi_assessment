Step 4: Reasoning discipline

We count a lab's own disclosure of an internal model as evidence against keeping that capability quiet, not for it, unless it shows a capability level beyond the public frontier on the parts that hold A down (long projects, reliability, learning on the job, generalization); today's accounts don't (our inference). Unreviewed preprints and company blogs are rated expert opinion or credible report unless their claims are the source's own, checkable facts, and thin markets are prices, not evidence (our method).
