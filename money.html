Follow the money

Code can be hidden; money is harder to hide. Spending, revenue and markets might show a lab sitting on something far beyond its public models before any announcement. In an AI investment boom, a spending spike means "bubble" far more often than "hidden AGI".

What would be a red flag

Compute with no matching release, which is alarm condition X4.

Show the details
  • Compute with no matching release: an operating cluster or training run at least 2x Epoch AI's trend for the largest, with nothing publicly attributed to it and no model from it released or externally evaluated within 12 months (condition X4). Planned or announced capacity doesn't count.
  • Revenue mix shifting: revenue from selling access flattening while labs' own research, trading or products grow (W6 and the exclusive-use signal).
  • Insiders: heavy insider buying, or departures that give up equity with warnings attached.
  • Markets: a sharp repricing of AGI prediction markets with no public news behind it.

Money is context for our odds. On its own it sets no alarm condition; only X4, unexplained compute, can.
