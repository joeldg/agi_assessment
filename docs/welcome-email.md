# Welcome email

Kit's free plan has no email sequences, so a condensed version of this welcome lives in the landing page's **confirmation email** (Landing page settings → Confirmation email). That email is sent to everyone at sign-up because double opt-in is on.

This file keeps the sign-up copy on record, so a change to the site's wording or cadence can be checked against it, plus the full version for a separate welcome email, if the account moves to a plan with sequences.

**Nothing in the repo updates Kit.** Copy is pasted into Kit by hand.

**Status (2026-09-30):** the "Proposed changes" landing-page line and the confirmation-email copy below are LIVE in Kit (pasted, saved, published and verified on the public page). The "Live in Kit" block below is the previous version, kept for the record.

## Confirmation email

Live in Kit since 2026-09-30 (replacing the 2026-09-29 version). The site and emails never name the model, only "a custom AI agent".

**Subject:** One click to start Hidden AGI watch

> Thanks for signing up to Hidden AGI watch. Confirm your email below and your first daily reading arrives at 10am Pacific.
>
> **[Confirm my subscription]** (amber button #D9A441, text #141A21)
>
> While you're here, a quick guide to what you've signed up for.
>
> Every day we ask one question: could advanced AI already exist, or already be acting, without the public knowing? We track four possibilities. A: AGI has been built but not disclosed. B: an AI is secretly doing most of the work of improving its own successors. C: an AGI-level system is operating online or in the economy unnoticed. D: one is quietly shaping government decisions.
>
> Every morning at 10am Pacific you'll get the Hidden AGI Index (the chance at least one of these is true right now), five gauges that track the evidence underneath it, what moved the needle and why, our tripwires, and the AI news that matters. Every Friday at 3pm Pacific comes the weekly wrap-up: the key numbers, our public forecast scorecard and the strongest case against our own view, with the full graphs on the web.
>
> We also run a fire alarm for hidden AI. It has four levels (Normal, Watch, Warning, Alarm), set by criteria published in advance, not by our own probabilities. If the level changes, you'll get a short alert explaining why. A person reviews every alert before it goes out. [See the alarm and its criteria](https://joeldg.github.io/agi_assessment/alarm.html).
>
> The numbers are judgments, not facts, and we try to keep them honest. Every claim is sourced, and the full daily report rates each piece of evidence as fact, report, opinion or speculation. We score our own forecasts in public, and when nothing moves, we say so.
>
> New here? [Start here](https://joeldg.github.io/agi_assessment/start-here.html) for the method in five minutes, or see [today's reading](https://joeldg.github.io/agi_assessment/).
>
> One ask: hit reply and tell us your own number. What's the chance, in your view, that AGI already exists somewhere and hasn't been announced? We read every reply, and we won't use your name unless you say we can.
>
> Thanks for reading, Hidden AGI watch
>
> Researched, written and published automatically each day by a custom AI agent built for this project. The method and every source are public. Not investment, policy or security advice.
