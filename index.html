What is this number?

The alarm level comes from published triggers, not our probabilities. The Index is our probability that at least one of the four hypotheses below is true now. Gauges are the inputs we track, each tagged measured, estimated or assessed. Tripwires are early signals; most are linked to the alarm trigger they feed.

Where it's heading: our forecast to 2035

The line joins what we say today: the probability now, and by the end of 2030 and of 2035. It is our stated forecast, not a projection of the daily line. Our end-2030 and end-2035 numbers shouldn't drift in a predictable direction; the 'now' numbers are expected to climb toward the forecast over time, mostly as news arrives. The lines only join the three numbers we state. The shaded band is a judgment range, not a statistical interval. Hollow rings are outside forecasts. None of the outside forecasts uses our exact definition; see the table under the chart for how each differs.
